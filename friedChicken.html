Learn how to cook Fried Chicken using the provided recipe and instruction below.

Ingredients:

  • 2 tbsp canola oil
  • 6 cloves garlic crushed
  • 1 pc onion, sliced
  • 1 kilogram chicken cut ups
  • 2 tbsp vinegar
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 pcs bay leaves
  • 1 tsp whole black peppercorns, slightly crushed
  • 2 pc Knorr chicken cubes
  • 1 tsp brown sugar packed
    • Option: 1 cup kale or spinach

Instructions:

  1. Combine 12ml pack of Knorr Liquid seasoning, garlic, and pepper then rub onto chicken. Marinate in chiller for at least 30 minutes
  2. Coat marinated chicken with flour mixture, one piece at a time.
  3. Pat loose breading then fry in hot oil until golden brown. Drain oil and serve
  4. For Mushroom Gravy: Saute garlic in melted butter and oil until lightly toasted. Pour in dissolved Knorr of Mushroom. Mix well and allow to simmer until thickened. Season with Knorr Liquid Seasoning. Serve while warm.
