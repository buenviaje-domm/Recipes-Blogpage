Learn how to cook Pork Giniling using the provided recipe and instruction below.

Ingredients:

  • 1 1/2 lb ground pork
  • 1 1/2 cups potatoes diced
  • 1 cup carrots diced
  • 8 ounces tomato sauce
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Sugar
  • Beef or Pork cube
  • Boiled Eggs
  • Salt and pepper
  • Cooking oil
  • Water

Instructions:

  1. pour-in the cooking oil.
  2. put-in the garlic.
  3. Add onions
  4. Add ground pork.
  5. Add beef or pork cube
  6. Add carrot and tomatoes
  7. Add salt, sugar, black pepper and stir
  8. Add boiled eggs
